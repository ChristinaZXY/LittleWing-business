My baby's barely fed for two days and there's an ulcer-like spot on her tongue. What could be wrong?
Wingie
I can't diagnose, but feeding refusal plus a tongue sore is worth taking seriously. Common culprits include hand, foot and mouth disease, oral thrush, or a canker sore from teething. What worries me most is two days of poor feeding — babies can dehydrate fast, so I'd call your pediatrician today. Watch for fewer wet diapers, a dry mouth, or unusual sleepiness. About us

We're building the support parents have long been missing.

LittleWing started with a simple observation: parenting is full of questions, and the answers are scattered across forums, group chats, and half-remembered advice. We set out to gather that support into one calm place — a companion that learns your way, offers advice tailored to your child, and surfaces the right products and resources exactly when you need them.
